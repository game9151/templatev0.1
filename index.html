About Us

Built on Precision and Trust

Summit Auto Works is a local automotive workshop in Sydney, looking after drivers across the area. We started with a simple goal: to offer the skill of a specialist workshop with the honest, straightforward service you’d expect from a trusted local mechanic.

Our team works on all makes and models — from daily drivers and family SUVs to prestige and European vehicles. We invest in the right diagnostic equipment, quality parts and ongoing training, so every job is done properly the first time.

We believe in explaining the problem in plain English, quoting clearly up front and never doing work you haven’t approved. Our 5.0 Google rating reflects that commitment — and the trust our customers have placed in us.
